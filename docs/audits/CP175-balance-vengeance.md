## Eligibility and boundaries

The pinned balanceSpells membership includes Wrath, Starfire, Moonfire, Insect Swarm and Hurricane. Forever periodic crit rules apply to the inspected spells, none of which carries NoPeriodicCrit. All seven current local crit-capable effects correspond: Wrath impact, Starfire impact, Moonfire impact/tick, Insect Swarm tick, Hurricane primary/secondary tick. Existing local Hurricane secondary scheduling and Insect Swarm hit semantics remain unchanged. Faerie Fire, cooldowns and current combat-item effects are not crit-capable; Thorns bypasses this resolver.

Shared SpellRolls does not compose effect-level BonusCriticalDamagePercent; no current eligible effect sets that property. Future crit-capable effects or critical-bonus items require fresh eligibility/composition review. This patch does not alter shared/global critical infrastructure, Naturalist, Moonfury, Moonglow, Eclipse, Thorns, spell eligibility, talent admission, UI or presets. Moonglow's sourced8/17/25 rank table is a separately recorded later issue. Existing provisional spell tuning, generic periodic resolution and Omen approximations remain unchanged.
