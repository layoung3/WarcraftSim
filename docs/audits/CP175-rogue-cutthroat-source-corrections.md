## Preserved local conventions, not new source claims

The tooltip does not resolve every miss/avoidance/zero-damage case, refresh/charge policy, failed-start consumption rule, simultaneous-event ordering, true-Stealth interaction or multiplayer ownership rule. This stage preserves existing behavior rather than claiming the historical tooltip proves it:

- The processor's existing case-insensitive source-actor exclusion applies first. A positive, nonperiodic damage event with the exact `backstab` key and positive rank reaches one Cutthroat roll at its current location. Missing/zero rank and ineligible events consume no Cutthroat draw. No extra result-key, target, hand or deduplication filter is added.
- Full casts also consume hit/avoidance/crit and other eligible proc draws. An isolated processor-event one-draw test is not a one-draw-per-cast model. Cutthroat retains its place before later proc processing; changed outcomes may legitimately alter subsequent rotation and RNG consumption.
- A successful proc creates one actor-held opportunity. Refresh retains one opportunity, replaces runtime aura identity and schedules the new expiry; an older expiry event must not remove the refreshed aura. The opportunity is not tied to the Backstab target, while generated combo points remain target-owned.
- Aura activity is half-open. A proc at time 2 with the corrected duration is live at 11.999 and unavailable at 12 or 12.001. A successful refresh at time 5 moves its expiry to 15. Focused real in-process tests cover these boundaries and the stale scheduled expiry.
- Ambush still requires the existing legacy dagger/behind-target spellbook eligibility and live Stealth or Cutthroat at actual start. A stale enabled rotation entry cannot bypass the live aura check, input readiness, resources, GCD or target checks. Cutthroat does not enable Garrote or Cheap Shot.
- Completed Ambush removes the acting Rogue's Cutthroat before the damage result, including when Ambush misses. Rejected starts do not consume the opportunity. Existing true-Stealth interaction, Vanish suppression/re-enabling and the `StealthOpeners` completed-cast metric meaning remain intact.
- The generic source-aura gate is not newly restricted to own-source auras. Source-actor exclusion for generating events is not proof of universal foreign-aura exclusion. No Core ownership expansion or multiplayer certification is included.

Foreign actors/auras, retained expired objects, synthetic negative damage, two-target opportunity/CP behavior and a constructed parry fixture are in-process coverage, not invented public HTTP inputs. Public behind-target Backstab can miss or dodge but cannot parry; front positioning removes the ability.
